An Android app that gives you a small, meaningful way to connect with others based on the time of day.

## How it works
1. Enter time of day (Morning, Afternoon, Dinner...)
2. Tap "Get My Spark"
3. Get a kindness challenge to brighten someone's day

## Built With
- Android Studio
- Kotlin

## Why I built it
To help people fight loneliness and spread joy, one small spark at a time.
